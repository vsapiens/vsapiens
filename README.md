### 👋 Hey, I'm Erick

I'm a Software Engineer who landed in Performance Engineering — and never looked back. I spend my days making systems faster, diagnosing bottlenecks at the infrastructure level, and building backends with **Node.js/TypeScript** and **Python** that actually hold up under pressure. Lately, I've been diving deep into the AI space, working with **LangChain agent frameworks** and using LLMs to tackle data modeling problems in ways that would've taken weeks just a couple of years ago. Whether I'm profiling a slow query, wiring up an AI agent pipeline, or teaching someone how to debug a race condition — I'm most at home when the problem is hard and the solution has to be precise. Let's build something that doesn't fall over.
